Quarantine Analysis

2,299 records failed validation · Review, investigate, and remediate before re-ingestion

Remediation Plan: Fix SA ID and cellphone data at source

987 records have invalid SA ID numbers (43% of quarantined) — the root cause is likely upstream data entry validation gaps. 773 cellphone records are landline numbers in mobile fields. These two issues alone account for 77% of all quarantine volume. Fix these at the CRM/onboarding layer to significantly improve pipeline DQ score.
